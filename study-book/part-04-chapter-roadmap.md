# PART 4 — Chapter Roadmap

This Part shows what each chapter will contain, which official syllabus units it serves, and what must be checked against current sources before it is written. Topic lists for chapters not yet written are **plans**; they may be adjusted when the chapter is researched.

**Fixed size of every chapter:** each topic carries 5 labelled MCQs and 1 short-answer question; each chapter ends with 25 further MCQs and 5 short-answer questions.
