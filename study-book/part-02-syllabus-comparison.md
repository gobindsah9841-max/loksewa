## 2.2 The single most important finding

**The seven provinces do not share one Paper I pattern.** They fall into four groups.

| Group | Provinces | GK : Mental Ability | What makes the group different |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. 30 + 20, counted** | Madhesh, Gandaki, Sudurpashchim | 30 Q : 20 Q | Eleven GK units with the allocation 1-2-4-1-2-4-3-3-3-2-5. Marks count in the merit list. |
| **B. 30 + 20, screening only** | Lumbini, Karnali | 30 Q : 20 Q | The objective test only selects candidates for the next stage. **Its marks are not added to the final result.** |
| **C. GK-heavy with language** | Koshi | 35 Q : 15 Q | Separate Koshi Province unit; Nepali and English language units. |
| **D. GK-dominant with language and governance** | Bagmati | 40 Q : 10 Q | Governance unit; Nepali and English units; education and health unit. |

Three further findings change how this book must be used:

1. **Public Management and Office Management are not part of the objective Paper I in any of the seven syllabi read.** They sit in the subjective paper "Contemporary Studies and Public Service Management" (समसामयिक अध्ययन र सार्वजनिक सेवा व्यवस्थापन) and in the service-group paper. See section 2.7.
2. **In Karnali the words "First Paper" mean something different.** The objective GK and mental-ability test is called the **Preliminary Examination** (प्रारम्भिक परीक्षा). The "First Paper" (प्रथम पत्र) of Karnali is the *subjective* paper on Contemporary Studies and Public Service Management.
3. **The syllabi contain units that are not in sections A–L of the book plan:** the universe and world geography, world history, and (Koshi and Bagmati only) Nepali and English language. Chapters 19–21 are added to cover them.

## 2.5 Question formats

| Format | Where it is defined | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Level I** — 60% of GK (18 of 30 questions) | Madhesh, Gandaki, Lumbini, Sudurpashchim | Plain multiple-choice question with four options |
| **Level II** — 40% of GK (12 of 30 questions) | Madhesh, Gandaki, Lumbini, Sudurpashchim | Four options are still given, but the question is built as: true/false (ठिक-बेठिक), matching (जोडा मिलाउने), fill in the blank (खाली ठाउँ भर्ने), cause and effect (कारण र प्रभाव), odd one out (नमिल्दो छुट्याउने). Gandaki also lists arranging in sequence. |
| Model questions printed in the syllabus | Koshi | Sample questions, for example on the number of local levels in Koshi Province. **These are samples, not past questions.** |
| Not noted | Bagmati, Karnali | No Level I / Level II split was noted in the documents read |

**Consequence for practice.** In four provinces about **12 GK questions in every paper are not simple recall**. This book therefore gives a large share of matching, ordering, true/false and cause–effect questions. They are labelled `[Pattern-Based – Reconstructed]`, which means "written to the official format", not "taken from a past paper".

## 2.7 Where does Public Management sit?

| Province | In the objective test? | Where it is examined |
|---|---|---|
| Koshi | No | Phase 2 subjective paper |
| Madhesh | No | Paper II (Contemporary Studies and Public Service Management) and Paper III (service group: office operation, registration and dispatch, filing, note-writing, minutes, records) |
| Bagmati | No — but unit 1.8 tests the governance system objectively | Paper II and Paper III |
| Gandaki | No | Paper II and Paper III |
| Lumbini | No | Paper II and Paper III |
| Karnali | No — but plans, budget, revenue jurisdiction, e-service delivery and e-governance appear inside the GK units | **"First Paper"** and Second Paper (both subjective) |
| Sudurpashchim | No | Paper II and Paper III |

**How this book handles it.** Chapter 17 (Public Management and Office Management) is kept, because the book plan asks for it and because it is the core of the subjective stage. It is classified **Supplementary for the objective test**, and its questions are written so that they also prepare you for short subjective answers.

> **Do not confuse with technical posts.** For *technical* 5th-level posts (seen in Lumbini and Madhesh syllabi), Paper I contains a part called "General Awareness and Public Management". That is a different syllabus and is outside this book.

## 2.9 Province profile cards

**Koshi** — 35 GK + 15 mental ability. Learn the Koshi Province unit (2 questions) and the two language units (4 questions). Marks count in the merit list. One subjective paper only.

**Madhesh** — 30 GK + 20 mental ability; Level I 18 and Level II 12. Current affairs is the largest unit (5). Marks count in the merit list. *Confirm against the official PDF listed on 24 Mangsir 2082.*

**Bagmati** — 40 GK + 10 mental ability. The broadest GK syllabus: governance, education and health, disaster management and two language units. Mental ability is only one fifth of the paper. Marks count in the merit list.

**Gandaki** — 30 GK + 20 mental ability; Level I 60%, Level II 40%. Gandaki content sits inside the geography and culture units. Marks count in the merit list.

**Lumbini** — 30 GK + 20 mental ability; a separate Lumbini Province unit. **Screening test: pass mark 45%, marks not carried forward.** Laws are taken as at the date of advertisement. *Check the revision notice of 2 Ashoj 2083.*

**Karnali** — Preliminary Examination, 30 GK + 20 mental ability. **Screening test: marks not carried forward.** Distinctive content: Khas kingdom and Sinja civilisation; Karnali crops, herbs and mines; e-governance and cyber security; revenue jurisdiction.

**Sudurpashchim** — 30 GK + 20 mental ability; Level I 60%, Level II 40%. Sudurpashchim content sits inside geography, culture and economy. Marks count in the merit list.

## 2.11 Priority classification

**Basis.** The classification uses only the official question allocations in section 2.6. It is **not** based on counting past papers, because no official past paper could be verified for this edition (see Part 3, section 3.9).

| Priority | Meaning | Chapters | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Very High** | Six or more questions in most provinces | **18** Mental Ability; **7** Science, Technology and Environment; **9** Current Affairs | Mental ability 10–20 questions. Science plus environment: 6 in Koshi and in the eleven-unit provinces, about 13 in Bagmati. Current affairs: 5 in Koshi, Madhesh, Gandaki and Sudurpashchim. |
| **High** | Three to five questions | **1** Geography of Nepal; **6** Society, Culture and Heritage; **5** Economy and Public Finance; **2** History of Nepal; **your own province chapter** | Geography of Nepal 3–4; society 3–4; economy 3–5; history 2–4; own-province questions are explicit or embedded in five provinces |
| **Moderate** | One to three questions | **8** International Relations; **19** Universe and World Geography; **20** World History; **3** Constitution; **4** Federalism and Governance | International relations 2–3; universe plus world geography 3; world history 1; governance 3 in Bagmati |
| **Supplementary** | Not examined objectively, or relevant only to other provinces | **17** Public Management; the **six other** province chapters | Subjective stage only; other provinces matter only as national GK |
